Short Description

Horizon of Steel is a systems-deep action RPG where you don't fight the battles — you engineer the build that wins them. Command combat through stats, gear, skills, and talents, queue up your farming, and watch your machine of a character demolish the field on its own.

Long Description

Horizon of Steel takes the build depth of a hardcore ARPG — loot, affixes, crafting, sprawling skill and passive trees — and removes the twitch. Combat is hands-off: your decisions live on the character screen, and the battlefield executes them, deterministically, every time.

Engineer a character through the Positronic Grid, skills, conduits, affinity rings, subpaths, and factions. Then queue journeys and let them run — farm offline and return to loot, levels, and progress waiting for you. Every victory is a build victory. It's a theorycrafter's playground built for the players who love the spreadsheet as much as the fight.

Key Features

  • Hands-off combat — you build the character; the war fights on.
  • Deep build systems — grids, skills, conduits, affinities, subpaths, factions.
  • Queue & farm — stack journeys, progress offline, come back to rewards.
  • Meaningful loot — affixes, crafting, rarities, and sockets that reshape your build.
  • Deterministic & replayable — same build, same result; pure optimization.
